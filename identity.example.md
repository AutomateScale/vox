# Who I am (Vox identity notes)

Copy this file to `identity.md` and make it yours. Vox injects it into smart
replies (triple-tap), content expansion (Shift+hold), and "Hey Vox" answers —
so what the alien writes sounds like YOU and knows your world. It never leaves
your Mac (gitignored; travels only in your own brain export).

Keep it short — a few lines is plenty. Delete these instructions and write
your own:

Name and what you do — e.g. "Jane Rivera, founder of Acme Studio (acme.co).
Brand designer, 12 years, mostly SaaS and DTC clients."

People and brands worth spelling right — clients, partners, products,
recurring collaborators. (Vox also learns these from your dictation, but
naming them here helps from day one.)

Your voice — e.g. "Direct and warm. Short sentences. I sign off '— Jane'."

That's it. The more specific this is, the more the alien writes like you.
